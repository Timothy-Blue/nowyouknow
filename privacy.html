Privacy policy

Nowyouknow · Last updated 9 October 2026

Nowyouknow is a self-hosted publishing tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing Nowyouknow you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Nowyouknow receives an OAuth access token and refresh token. It uses them to upload videos, set video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, and read basic channel and video statistics for the owner's own channel.

How it is stored

Tokens and post data are stored only in the owner's self-hosted instance of the open-source scheduler Postiz, on a machine the owner controls. Nothing is sent to any third party other than the platforms the owner chooses to post to.

Sharing

Nowyouknow does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use the data for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. To revoke access at any time, remove "Nowyouknow" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting the channel in Postiz deletes the stored tokens.
